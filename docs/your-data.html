Your data and privacy

Everything personal is stored only on your computer, in a folder called .stock-analyst in your home folder:

  • Mac / Linux: ~/.stock-analyst/
  • Windows: C:\Users\<your name>\.stock-analyst\
FileWhat's in it
profile.mdHow you invest: markets, style, horizon, preferences
lessons.mdWhat reviews and your corrections taught it
journal.jsonlEvery call: date, price, verdict, reason
portfolio.jsonYour holdings
watchlist.jsonSymbols saved in the dashboard
peers.jsonThe rivals you chose to compare each stock with
screens.jsonYour saved screens: their filters, extra symbols and the stocks that matched at the last check
reports/Full reports Claude wrote, one Markdown file per ticker and date
config.jsonYour custom factor weights and AI settings (only if you changed any)
changes.mdA history of weight changes
cache.dbSaved market data and headlines, plus a daily history of each stock's score. Safe to delete; it's rebuilt
risk.jsonYour day-trading limits (account size, risk per trade, daily loss limit)
daytrades.jsonlDay trades you logged in the Trading desk: profit or loss, and optionally entry, exit, stop, setup and a note
alerts.jsonYour price alerts
options/The Trading desk's options history: one file per stock with each day's option prices, Delta, Gamma and IV near the stock's price, kept 180 days
practice.jsonYour Paper trade account: pretend cash, holdings, orders and its value by day
rewind.jsonYour Rewind rounds: each stock and date, your call, how sure you were, and the result
research.jsonYour random participant code (like P-7K3Q9X), and if you join a study, your study code, check-in answers and daily counts. Leave the study clears the study parts
welcome.jsonThat you've read the welcome screen
license.jsonThe email and key from your invitation, a random id for this computer, and when the key was last checked
analytics.jsonWhether you chose to share usage, and if so a random id for this computer
progress.jsonYour Start-here progress, and whether the Trading desk is turned on
logs/dashboard.logTechnical details of problems (errors and their traces), for troubleshooting. Rotated at 1 MB; safe to delete
keys.envYour free AI keys, if you added any (see Free AI reports)

Good to know​

  • Updates never touch this folder. It's kept separate from the skill on purpose.
  • You can read and edit these files with any text editor. profile.md and lessons.md are plain text.
  • Back it up by copying the folder somewhere safe.
  • Start fresh by deleting the folder. It's recreated automatically, empty.
  • Move it by setting the environment variable STOCK_ANALYST_DATA to another folder path.

What leaves your computer?​

  • Ticker symbols are sent to Yahoo Finance to fetch prices. Your holdings and quantities are not sent.
  • Your conversation with Claude goes to Anthropic, as with any Claude conversation. Claude reads your profile, lessons and journal to tailor its answers.
  • Web searches for full reports go through Claude's web search tool.
  • Your invitation key: about once a day, the app sends your email, key, app version and a random id for this computer to the licence server, which answers whether the key is good. Nothing else goes with it.
  • Usage, only if you said yes: which tabs and features you use and which symbols you open; for paper trades, only how you traded (buy or sell, stock or option, US or Indian market, where the order started, the lesson it practised, roughly how long a holding was kept, and why an order was refused), never the symbol, amount, price or result; with the app version, your operating system and a random id for this computer, sent to Google Analytics. Never your email, amounts, holdings, notes or anything you type. Change your answer under How to use (the ? button).
  • News for Today: to find news, the app searches Google News for the names of up to 8 stocks you hold or watch, asks the GDELT news index and the Federal Reserve's press feed about market-wide topics, and asks the SEC for recent filings of US stocks you follow (with the identification set in config.json, as SEC requires). Only the stock names and symbols go out, never amounts, notes or your email.
  • Update check: about every four hours, the app asks the place you installed from (the link in your invitation) for its list of files, to see whether a newer version is out. It sends nothing about you — just a plain request for that list.
  • Free AI reports (optional) send only public market data and headlines to the AI provider you set up. Your holdings, journal, profile and lessons are never sent. See Free AI reports.
