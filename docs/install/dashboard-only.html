Install the dashboard (no Claude plan needed)

Most of Chatoor works without Claude Code: the Scanner, Journal, Rewind, Paper trade, Learn and the Trading desk. This guide installs that dashboard by pasting one line. You don't need a GitHub account, Python or any other setup. You won't get Claude's written full reports or chatting about stocks; for those, follow the full install instead.

It takes about 5 minutes, most of it waiting for downloads. You need an internet connection, a Mac or a Windows 10 or 11 computer, and the install line from your invitation email. While Chatoor is in its pilot, the download link is only shared with testers, so it isn't on this page.

On a Mac​

  1. Open Terminal. Press ⌘ Command + Space, type Terminal, and press Return. A window with a blinking cursor opens. You'll only use it to paste one line.

  2. Copy the Mac line from your invitation. It's one long line that looks like this:

    curl -LsSf https://gist.githubusercontent.com/…/raw/install.sh | sh

    Copy all of it, from curl to sh.

  3. Paste it into Terminal with ⌘ Command + V, then press Return.

  4. Wait. You'll see lines like:

    Installing uv, which runs the app (about a minute)...
    Downloading Chatoor 0.4.0...
    Chatoor 0.4.0 is installed.
    Open it any time by double-clicking "Chatoor" on your desktop.
    Opening it now. The first start downloads a few Python packages, so give it a minute.

    Nothing asks for a password. If a line starts with "Chatoor install stopped", see If something goes wrong.

  5. Chatoor opens. On most Macs it opens in its own window; on other computers (and older Macs) it opens in your web browser at http://127.0.0.1:8787. Either way it runs on your own computer, and the address only works for you.

  6. Enter your email and key. The first time, the app asks for the email your invitation was sent to and the key in it (like SA-K7QM-2XPA-9RTD-WC4H; capitals and dashes don't matter). It checks them online, then opens. A key works on up to three computers.

On Windows​

  1. Open PowerShell. Click Start, type PowerShell, and click Windows PowerShell. A blue or black window with a blinking cursor opens.

  2. Copy the Windows line from your invitation. It looks like this:

    powershell -ExecutionPolicy ByPass -c "irm https://gist.githubusercontent.com/…/raw/install.ps1 | iex"

    Copy all of it, from powershell to iex".

  3. Paste it into PowerShell by right-clicking inside the window (or Ctrl + V), then press Enter.

  4. Wait for "Chatoor 0.4.0 is installed." A second window opens and starts the app.

  5. Chatoor opens in your web browser at http://127.0.0.1:8787.

  6. Enter your email and key from your invitation, as on a Mac.

On Linux​

Paste the Mac line into a terminal. Afterwards, open the app with the stock-analyst command (the installer prints its full path if your terminal doesn't know it).

Opening it again later​

Double-click Chatoor on your desktop.

  • On most Macs it opens in its own window, with the Chatoor icon in the Dock and the usual menu bar (⌘Q to quit, ⌘C/⌘V to copy and paste). It reopens at the size and place you last left it. Close the window to stop it; your data is saved.
  • Otherwise (older Macs, Windows, Linux) it opens in your browser, after a small window that keeps it running. Keep that small window open while you use Chatoor; closing it stops the app. On a Mac that small window is Terminal, which you can close with ⌘ Command + Q when you're done.

Updating​

Paste the same line from your invitation again. It installs the newest version and keeps your journal, watchlist and holdings. On Windows, if Chatoor is open, the installer closes it first; open it again from the desktop afterwards.

If something goes wrong​

The installer says what went wrong and what to do. The usual ones:

  • "That email and key don't match": check both against your invitation. The key works with or without dashes and in any case. "Already in use on 3 computers", "turned off" or "This key ended on" a date: contact the person who invited you. For a key that was turned off or has ended, the key screen also shows Remove the app from this computer, which explains the uninstall line below. "Couldn't check your key for 14 days": connect to the internet and reopen the app.
  • "couldn't reach the download page" or "the download didn't finish": check your internet connection, then paste the line again. Work or school networks sometimes block downloads; try at home.
  • "doesn't match its published checksum": the download was damaged or changed on the way, so nothing was installed. Try again; if it keeps happening, tell us.
  • "uv didn't install": close the window, open a new Terminal or PowerShell, and paste the line again.
  • The browser says "This site can't be reached": the small Chatoor window was closed. Double-click the desktop icon again.
  • "doesn't know where to download from": you used an installer from somewhere else. Use the line from your invitation.
  • Windows shows "running scripts is disabled": you pasted only part of the line. Copy the whole line, starting with powershell.

Still stuck? Copy everything in the window and send it to us. It doesn't contain anything personal.

Removing it​

Paste your install line from the invitation again, but change the word install to uninstall near the end:

  • Mac: …/raw/install.sh | sh becomes …/raw/uninstall.sh | sh, pasted into Terminal.
  • Windows: …/raw/install.ps1 | iex" becomes …/raw/uninstall.ps1 | iex", pasted into PowerShell.

It stops Chatoor if it's running, removes the app and its desktop icon, and then asks whether to delete your data too (your journal, watchlist, holdings and any saved AI keys). Press Return (or Enter) to keep it, or type y first to delete it. Kept data comes back if you install again later.

uv, the small tool the installer added to run the app, stays: other programs can use it.
