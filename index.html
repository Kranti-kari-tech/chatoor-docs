Chatoor by Krantikari

Learn to read a stock, not to follow tips

Chatoor explains what a company’s numbers mean, lets you practise on real past prices, and keeps score of how your calls — and its own — turn out. It is a place to learn to analyse, not to be told what to buy.

Everything a careful investor does, and nothing a tipster does

A score you can read

Chatoor rates a company on seven measures — value, growth, profitability, health, momentum, analysts and earnings — each in plain words. It describes the data; it never tells you what to buy.

How the score works

Practise before it is real

Rewind shows a real stock from years ago with its name hidden: make your call and see what happened in seconds. Paper trade lets you buy and sell with pretend money, and keeps nothing at risk.

Rewind and paper trade

A journal that keeps score

Every call is checked after 30 days against the market. You see how often you were right, next to how often the tool was, so you learn where your judgement is strong and where it is not.

How it learns

A calm start to each day

Open Chatoor and the first screen each day is Today: the stocks you follow, the news that may matter and why, and one idea to think about. It compares the buzz with what the data shows, so a busy headline becomes something to study, never a tip to act on.
